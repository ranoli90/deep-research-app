# Strong areas to preserve

The audit should not trigger a rewrite of working foundations.

## Strong / materially good
- Fenced worker writes: lease, account, consent, revision and cancellation checks.
- Run dispatch outbox + lease model.
- SSRF protection: pinned resolution, private/reserved IP rejection, redirect revalidation, remote-address verification, byte/header limits.
- Typed correction child runs and evidence inheritance without automatic conclusion inheritance.
- Evidence selection immutable proof and inventory gates.
- Exact quote/span validation and ownership checks.
- Account/source tombstones and broad deletion cleanup.
- Provider receipt retention/reconciliation concept.
- Model output schemas and evidence-handle binding.
- Calculation derivation tamper checks.
- Requested-verification target lineage/digest checks.
- Honest refusal to merge `main` while current PG is red.
- Preservation of historical unknown holds.

Fix around these systems; do not simplify them away.
