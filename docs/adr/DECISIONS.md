# Architecture decision register
Reviewed: 2026-09-16. All decisions are proposed for implementation; none implies a migrated live application.

## ADR-009 — measured offline HTML extraction (2026-09-17 implementation checkpoint)

Select Trafilatura 2.2.0 in an isolated Python process behind the existing TypeScript worker. Keep lxml table cells, spans and linked/adjacent footnotes because XML extraction alone drops some table geometry. Do not add Readability as a second production fallback. On identical saved bytes, the Readability 0.6.0/jsdom 26.1.0 candidate preserved 10/11 selected real-document spans; Trafilatura preserved 11/11, including PostgreSQL's connect-timeout caveat. One additional oversized download failed and stays in the denominator. The original eight synthetic cases scored 8/8 for the initial DOM candidate and 7/8 for vanilla Trafilatura (script-shell failure). The production wrapper fixes the shell and is tested separately. Agent-inspected references are not independent human adjudication or a quality leaderboard.

Tradeoff: adds a pinned Python runtime and Linux namespace requirement. The measured caveat loss and lower extraction times justify this small adapter/runtime addition, without replacing backend orchestration. Exact versions, hashes, failure records and retention notes are in `verification/v6/extraction/`; no third-party full documents are committed. The actual adapter preserved 11/11 saved real-document spans, with partial coverage warnings and roughly 0.65–1.59 seconds per isolated invocation on this machine. Reconsider when a broader held-out corpus shows omission, deployment compatibility fails, or runtime cost materially exceeds the benefit. PDF/OCR remains unimplemented. Rollback disables reading; it cannot label raw HTML or snippets as full text.

## ADR-001 — one backend codebase, separate API/worker processes
Choose modular monolith with three shared packages only when they contain real code. Keep original native/server/Supabase/Render direction. Reject separate provider/evidence/billing microservices at launch. Rationale: the first risk is demonstrable research usefulness and reliable transactions, not independent team deployment.
Strongest objection: retrieval/parser workloads may need resource isolation. Separate constrained extraction runtime when actual parsing risk requires it, without turning every domain into a service. Reverse when measured resource/security/deployment requirements justify separation. Port boundaries make reversal moderate, not zero cost. Rollback retains data contracts and fails closed.

## ADR-002 — relational evidence and decision dependencies
Choose typed relationships among constraints, questions, candidate selection, source versions, passages, claims, calculations and report blocks. Reject graph database and universal semantic graph initially. Rationale: selective repair needs ownership and dependency semantics, not a new storage paradigm.
Strongest objection: implicit dependencies are easy to miss. Use conservative branch/full invalidation when dependency completeness is unknown and reopen candidate discovery on relaxed constraints. Reverse to more advanced graph tooling only after query/maintenance limits are measured. Content versions remain subject to deletion.

## ADR-003 — bounded controller with optional escalation
Choose one iterative controller, combined investigation behavior and separate publication checks. Six logical concerns may share calls. Critics/parallel investigators are conditional, capped experiments. Keep one hosted provider route as an evaluation baseline, not invented internal visibility.
Strongest objection: parallel decomposition can improve hard fact gathering (S32). Allow a tested independent-question branch under the same atomic budget; preserve it only when matched evaluation shows benefit. No model vote is independent evidence. Reversal is inexpensive at strategy interface, but output/evidence contracts remain stable.

## ADR-004 — revision-fenced publication
Choose revision basis with brief/evidence/consent/cancel epochs plus worker fencing. Validate at tool authorization and atomic publication. Reject last-write-wins final reports and treating a client stream as state.
Strongest objection: stricter fencing can discard expensive late output. Permit safe raw receipt reconciliation without publishing obsolete conclusions; count actual provider cost honestly. Do not trade correctness/privacy for sunk cost. Migration requires contract compatibility mapping if an app exists.

## ADR-005 — outcome benchmark before broad feature growth
Choose two launch jobs plus correction lifecycle, with independent decisive-evidence rubrics. Reject a large unreviewed synthetic leaderboard and shell-first completion theater. Keep proposed 95/90 citation targets labeled unvalidated; add denominators and source-discovery/repair metrics.
Strongest objection: narrow tasks may not predict consumer demand. Recruit users with recent decisions and test both usefulness and return behavior; pivot the task focus rather than adding every category. A failed experiment is a reason to remove complexity, not relabel the metric.

## ADR-006 — private-data retention outranks historical immutability
Choose logically versioned reports but purge/redact affected private content when required. Keep non-content tombstones and permitted audit metadata. Reject retaining old private excerpts under an “immutable report” exception.
Strongest objection: source removal reduces reproducibility. Disclose that tradeoff and preserve only permitted metadata. External copies already downloaded cannot be recalled; make this limitation explicit. No rollback can restore access after revocation/deletion without lawful new authorization.

## ADR-007 — local correctness and gated progression
Choose real local PostgreSQL/queue for P0-D when cloud access is unavailable; separate live-provider and native evidence as P0-L/P0-N. Make P0 the first checkpoint without postponing minimum security, accessibility or governance. Reject both cloud-credentials-as-a-universal-blocker and treating a fixture/local pass as production compatibility.
Strongest objection: local environments can drift from production. Pin versions and migrations, record permissions/connection mode, and run hosted auth/RLS/storage/pooler/parity checks before deployment. Reverse the local test environment when a measured incompatibility demands it, not because a managed brand is assumed necessary. Existing safety/publication contracts remain unchanged.

## ADR-008 — notification identity without an exactly-once delivery claim
Choose a durable logical completion identity `(runId, completionEpoch)`, created atomically with publication, plus per-account/device-binding delivery identity. Provider deduplication/collapse features are capabilities, not assumptions. Reject the external review's claim that a local key alone prevents a duplicate OS alert after a send/receipt crash. Expo documents possible duplicated or missing handoff; a successful receipt is not proof of device delivery (N01–N03 in `research/RECONCILIATION_SOURCES.json`).
Strongest objection: suppressing an unknown-outcome retry can lose an optional alert. Prefer an inspectable in-app completion and reliable foreground refresh; default to no blind resend after an unreconcilable issued attempt. Change retry policy only with documented provider guarantees and a tested product tradeoff. No rollback may weaken account binding or disclose report contents. P0 tests local outbox invariants; live push arrives with P3 if enabled.
## ADR-010 — verify current Supabase identity before resolving local ownership

Use the existing Supabase direction through its documented Auth user endpoint, pinned to a configured HTTPS project origin. The server does not parse client metadata into authority or implement JWT cryptography. A bounded request occurs outside database transactions; the returned confirmed user subject/issuer maps to a local owner under an advisory/account lock. No cache of positive identity results is introduced. This trades one Auth request per API request for current provider-side user/session checking; hosted latency and failure behavior still require measurement. The immutable digest mapping prevents a still-valid provider token from recreating a locally deleted account. Production account creation grants no spending allowance. Local transport/database tests do not establish hosted authentication or provider-side deletion, which remain activation gates. No new library or service was adopted.

Sources inspected 2026-09-17: [Supabase getUser](https://supabase.com/docs/reference/javascript/auth-getuser), [JWT verification endpoint](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/jwts), and [session verification guidance](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/server-side/advanced-guide). Reverse this choice only with measured Auth latency/availability evidence and equivalent revocation/deletion guarantees; never fall back to development session hashes in production.


## ADR-011 — binary PDFs with measured physical text order

Adopt Docling Parse 7.20.0 behind the existing isolated extraction adapter, with pypdf 6.19.0 used only for strict metadata/page-count/encryption checks. Baseline pypdf text, PDFium text order and Docling's model-free native pipeline failed to preserve a decisive negation in an actual 46-page RFC PDF. Grouping Docling cells by their physical line coordinates repaired that case and passed an original mixed-font-order regression. Persist original cell geometry and page/block locators; no semantic quote repair. Measurements, exact hashes/licenses/dependencies and failures are in verification/v6/documents/. No ML models, OCR, full Docling orchestration or additional service is adopted. This adds Python/native parser dependencies because the smaller tested baselines lost material meaning. The diagnostic ran within the existing 512 MiB/15 CPU-second isolation limits. PDF table structure/column reading order remains unverified and output stays partial. Broader independent document evaluation may reverse this choice; rollback returns unavailable and preserves byte/deletion/publication gates. Never use raw parse success as a quality claim.


## ADR-012 — credential-wide reservation buckets without storing credentials

OpenRouter issuance records SHA-256 of the provider namespace and trimmed server credential on provider intents. This opaque identity scopes an independent operator cap across account/project buckets; it is never exposed in client contracts or logs. Server configuration LIVE_KEY_SPEND_CAP_MICRO defaults to zero and is not itself evidence of new spending authorization. Lock order is account, run, allowance, key bucket, project bucket; no network I/O occurs within the transaction. Additive migration014 leaves historical identity null because today's credential cannot prove which key paid an old intent. Such unattributed costs count conservatively against every key until evidence-based reconciliation; do not silently migrate them to zero or infer a key. Separate actual keys retain distinct buckets but still share the project limit. This can over-reserve historical cost; availability is the explicit tradeoff. No new dependency/service. Rollback disables issuance while retaining ledger identities/holds. Automatic receipt reconciliation and bounded model route estimates remain separate work.


## ADR-013 — one strict structured-model boundary

Replace the unused permissive proposal adapter with a typed OpenRouter transport and one durable worker coordinator for brief, extraction, support proposals, action proposals, writing and coverage review. Model outputs carry local keys and exact evidence spans; server ownership, budgets and publication remain independent. Canonical Zod3 schemas generate provider JSON Schema with exact ISC zod-to-json-schema3.25.1 (no runtime dependencies beyond the installed Zod peer); this avoids a hand-maintained second schema. Do not add an agent framework.

The initial text-only policy pins OpenAI/gpt-4o-mini with provider fallback/plugins disabled and maximum price filters. Public endpoint metadata and official routing/usage docs were checked; no paid capability or semantic probe was executed. A conservative full-advertised-context reserve fits existing allowance without raising it, but actual receipts and explicit unknowns remain authoritative. The strategy stays disabled pending full workflow integration and authorized evidence. Model failures never become fixture output. Versioned results persist only under consent/ownership/revision/fence checks and are deleted with the account. Fences do not suppress already-incurred financial receipts.

Regressions include corrupt cached receipts, duplicate concurrent proposals, stale evidence, missing current downstream consent, cross-owner passages and delete/lease takeover during a returned result. Exact license/integrity/audit and metadata are in verification/v6/gateway/. Seven pre-existing Expo-chain audit findings remain open. Reversal is a different adapter behind the same contracts or removal of the converter during a measured Zod4 migration; rollback disables issuance, never weakens evidence or privacy gates. This is gateway implementation, not a completed general research engine.


## ADR-014 — stable task interpretation before evidence-driven execution

Persist one versioned task interpretation per run/brief revision, derived from a validated brief operation. Allocate criterion/question identities on the server; retain model-local keys only as proposal mappings. Evidence arrival is not a reason to reinterpret the user's requirements or pay for the same brief again. Recover an existing successful proposal after a crash, verify its receipt/schema/prompt/policy binding on every load, and require a distinct task for a distinct brief revision.

Use one additive PostgreSQL table with owned JSON specification/ID maps, rather than another service or task graph database. Existing fenced transactions serialize adoption; unique(run,brief revision) prevents duplicates. Material ambiguity remains explicit. This does not certify model completeness or implement support/coverage. Reversal is a measured relational normalization behind the same task service; rollback disables preparation and retains deletion/accounting gates. ProcessRun integration and typed correction interpretation remain open.


ADR-013 input-membership amendment: persist a bounded metadata manifest of the exact model context alongside the existing result, rather than storing another full prompt/source copy or inferring attempted evidence from output citations. Unmentioned passages still belong to the attempted input. Historical missing manifests are unknown, not synthesized during migration. Reuse checks the exact manifest; this intentionally blocks old cached operations until explicitly handled, without automatic paid resend. No claim of full-document coverage or semantic verification follows from selection.


## ADR-015 — executed scoped support, separate from final publication

Use a fallible structured evidence-comparison operation plus independent deterministic binding/scope/numeric/access/contradiction/qualification checks. The resolver inspects all selected in-scope passages, not only the model's chosen quotes. Preserve conflicts and unknowns instead of collapsing them into a progress event. Legitimate paraphrases can pass the combined check; no lexical-overlap score proves entailment. Semantic quality still requires held-out real-model/reference evidence.

Persist canonical claim revisions and typed checks behind existing fences in two small PostgreSQL relations, reusing source/model/task identities. Keep claims unverified at the publication boundary until the report service validates the full report mapping and current basis. Rollback disables the new strategy; never restore overlap-only publication or delete receipt/privacy safeguards. No new model/service/dependency is required; a later checker version or processor must produce separately versioned evidence, not silently reinterpret prior results.


ADR-015 publication amendment: the report gate now recomputes and validates stored scoped results instead of trusting caller flags or forcing a valid semantic paraphrase back through the literal-only checker. Managed assertions never fall back when their scoped result is absent/non-supporting/stale. Pure compilation still requires exact final assertion mappings; new writer wording needs a new support path. Canonical assertion IDs/revisions survive publication/reopen. Policy constants remain server-owned in a backend port, unchanged; validation is read-only until the existing final publication decision passes.


## ADR-016 — final writer text is a new checked assertion

Do not restrict synthesis to copying source assertions or let new prose inherit their approval. Project each material draft surface into a new inference proposal with explicit source-premise revision links, check it against original evidence, and publish only supported wording through the existing canonical gate. Standard application headings remain nonfactual; unchecked headings/limitations cannot bypass this process. Unsupported draft surfaces become localized abstentions. The generated title is not exposed as a canonical factual field.

A single immutable draft-lineage relation ties existing operation/task IDs together; parent origin must be extraction, preventing recursive/self-supported drafts. Common premise scope is retained; mixed fields remain unknown and dependency completeness is partial. This supports conservative later correction invalidation rather than claiming complete causal dependencies. Model schema/prompt/provider remain unchanged. Checker v2 adds currency-token preservation; result identity now includes checker version so old judgments remain historical. Rollback disables the structured writer while preserving reports, deletion, reservations and versioned records.


## ADR017 — Criterion coverage is a persisted checked outcome
Decision: execute review_coverage against current owned task/evidence and revalidated assertion support. Persist the actual resolved question statuses, failed guards and canonical assertion revisions under both checker versions. Question coverage is distinct from candidate eligibility; model not-applicable labels cannot waive user requirements.
Consequence: no source-count completion; semantic review still needs real evaluation. Local additive migration021 only; no hosted deployment. Rollback disables new coverage execution while retaining privacy, holds and existing evidence. Main worker/publication integration remains open.


## ADR018 — Fixed public search policy with durable results
Pin one Exa auto plugin search through the existing bounded OpenRouter transport and pinned OpenAI text provider. Require versioned disclosure and default-off activation; reserve the documented plugin fee plus the full model ceiling. No model-directed multi-search server tool. Persist results separately from financial receipts, keyed to the logical query/brief/policy, so evidence arrival cannot justify resending an unknown request. Mixed document/public queries remain gated pending explicit approval. Local migration022 only; no paid behavior or invoice proof. Rollback disables new discovery and retains ownership, receipts, holds and deletion.


## ADR019 — Durable reads separate discovery metadata from evidence
Persist a source-read operation and its exact extracted version before treating a discovered URL as evidence. Keep search snippets separately labeled; source existence grants no answer coverage. Stable run/source/brief/reader identity permits replay without repeat network requests, while unknown interrupted reads remain unresolved. Reuse the existing safe transport and isolated extraction instead of adding another browser/parser runtime. Additive migration023 shares account deletion and worker fencing; no network transaction. Reverse only behind the same ownership/access/evidence contracts; rollback disables discovery and retains safety gates. Additional read-attempt recovery, adaptive discovery and correction membership remain open.


ADR019 criterion-loop amendment: use a small pure bounded policy to narrow search to unresolved criterion provenance before adding another model planning call. Every evidence change triggers actual assertion/support/coverage work; durable route limits prevent restart or concurrency from expanding query count. This approach cannot invent synonyms or search terms absent from the user question, and may exhaust without a distinct query. Broader approved query formulation needs measured evaluation and explicit privacy design. Rollback disables structured discovery, retaining safe evidence and accounting.


## ADR020 — Explicit replacement patches with immutable evidence membership
Use a full replacement question as the first typed correction, preserving the accepted user message and named parent revision. This avoids interpreting a contradiction-laden appended question as a settled patch. Conservative recomputation is explicit because dependency completeness is unknown; old approvals never transfer. Fine-grained criterion and formatting patches remain separate unfinished capabilities.
Reuse source snapshots through digest-bound passage membership and a common authorized read projection, rather than duplicating source rows or changing their owner/run. Snapshot and refresh are explicit policies. New discovery reopens conservatively for public tasks; private-query boundaries remain. Account deletion overrides history. Atomic idempotent admission includes allowance and dispatch outbox. Rollback disables typed admission while retaining membership reads, deletion and publication safeguards; no destructive down migration or hosted rollout is authorized.


ADR020 comparison amendment: derive a bounded exact inventory comparison from published claim revisions, criterion definitions and cited source versions, inside the publication transaction. Do not pay a model to infer a diff or treat the accepted patch/reuse flag as an observed outcome. Preserve actual revision IDs and measured snapshot membership. Exact wording/scope equality is explicitly narrower than semantic equivalence; missing history produces no comparison. Candidate eligibility changes need separate executed outcomes. The existing report JSON field stores this additive versioned result; no extra service or table.


ADR020 mobile adoption amendment: expose configured correction mode and reserved allowance on the existing owned run snapshot, rather than making mobile infer engine availability from a label or credential. Send the full question patch through the shared schema; retain snapshot/refresh as an explicit choice. Missing capability is unavailable. Native interaction remains a separate gate. The build repair uses the [documented Metro resolver hook](https://metrobundler.dev/docs/configuration/#resolverequest) only for public workspace contracts’ NodeNext relative specifiers; it delegates to normal Metro resolution and does not add a general alias/fallback layer.

## ADR021 — isolate historical controller from production composition (2026-09-17)
The production worker previously imported fixture adapters at module load and could fall back to the bounded composer when structured processing was disabled. Move that historical controller behind an explicit development-only entrypoint, preserving its tests as diagnostic evidence. Extract only the shared lease/lifecycle preflight and queue runtime composition; production research stays on the structured pipeline. Enforce the transitive runtime source graph at governance checks and exercise disabled/fixture behavior against PostgreSQL. No package, provider, schema, migration or spending change. Disabling production processing yields explicit unresolved failure for existing controlled jobs and rejects new production admission. Rollback keeps production disabled; the old controller is not a rollback research provider.

## ADR022 — deterministic recorded-scope comparison (2026-09-17)
Implement compare_scopes outside model prose and persist exact claim/evidence/support identities. Compare recorded fields conservatively: unknown stays unknown; an exact match does not establish proposition or quantity equivalence. Feed the typed result to the existing writer rather than introducing another model role. Version the result and the comparison-bearing model input manifest. Additive migration025 shares account deletion. Existing v1 writer contexts remain historical; new context adoption is blocked in code over unresolved older writer attempts. Pin representation per result via migration026 and versioned input identity so older known or unknown requests retain their body/manifest semantics. Exact compression bytes are engineering evidence only; model comprehension and research quality remain unevaluated. No budget or provider change; rollback disables new processing, preserving both representations and deletion.


## ADR023 — evidence-bound exact arithmetic handler
Use exact rational arithmetic over supported, uniquely located printed quantities rather than model arithmetic or arbitrary expression evaluation. Retain numerator/denominator and explicit assumptions; refuse ambiguous/qualified inputs and implicit unit conversion. Bind durable results to ordered operands, exact claim revisions, authorized evidence and checker versions, and recompute on restoration. This proves arithmetic only; matching units do not prove comparable scope or a valid real-world aggregate. Keep publication unchanged until a dedicated proof-aware derivation path can revalidate these records. Additive migration027 is owned by scoped evidence and purged on deletion. Rollback disables invocation while retaining evidence, deletion and safety gates. No external library, model call, service or hosted migration is introduced.


ADR023 publication amendment: use a distinct calculation-to-claim mapping and exact versioned renderer, then restore the arithmetic proof at the real report gate. Do not pretend the derived numeric output appears in a source or relax literal support globally. Preserve visible assumptions/input statements and exact fractions; record inference and arithmetic-specific checker provenance. Keep the final question-coverage gate unchanged until derived results are explicitly integrated into structured writing/coverage. Migration028 is private derived evidence and purged before input proofs on deletion.


ADR023 planning amendment: introduce a separately named/versioned calculation planning operation rather than altering existing saved action/writer request schemas. Use the same bounded gateway, not another provider or agent role runtime. Model output selects references; deterministic code restores supported quantities and executes exact arithmetic. Numeric evidence is necessary to invoke planning but does not prove arithmetic is needed or that its result answers a question. Existing admitted writers without a plan preserve their original path. Writer and coverage adoption remain a subsequent required integration, never inferred from a progress event.

ADR023 writer/coverage amendment: keep source assertions and arithmetic proofs distinct in model context and report publication. Give new writer/review operations separate schema/prompt identities so older requests retain their exact bodies. Let the model select known calculation keys, then render numbers and assumptions deterministically and revalidate exact content at publication. Store plan/draft/review lineage in migration029 and purge dependent derived content when its provenance is removed. Corrections recompute results over explicitly reused immutable source versions. Do not treat an exact sum as population comparability, eligibility or proof that the selected formula answers the question. Preliminary discovery still uses source-only coverage; semantic applicability, latency and held-out quality remain measured follow-up work.


## ADR024 — native binary document acquisition

Native document input decision, W04/W07: use expo-document-picker14.0.8 and expo-file-system19.0.24 behind the mobile account/request boundary and existing binary API. Both exact package manifests declare MIT; picker has Expo peer only, filesystem has Expo/React Native peers and was already in the lockfile. No model assets or new JavaScript production transitive dependencies. Versions match installed Expo54 bundledNativeModules. Reviewed Android/iOS picker source: copyToCacheDirectory creates a dedicated DocumentPicker cache copy. Read it with File.bytes(), check size before/after, delete that copy; never delete original URI. Official API references: https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v54.0.0/sdk/document-picker/ and https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v54.0.0/sdk/filesystem/ . Local audit returns seven existing moderate/high advisories (image-size/postcss/uuid); no clean-audit claim. Runtime cost: OS picker/cache copy plus a bounded8MiB byte read/upload, no provider charge. Account changes discard late selection; transient bytes are excluded from persistence. Native and signed-build evidence remain separate.

Native dependency review adds an unresolved build gate: picker Android declares androidx.annotation1.0.0 and commons-io2.6; existing filesystem declares commons-codec1.10, commons-io1.4, OkHttp4.9.2, Okio2.9.0, legacy-support-v4:1.0.0 and documentfile1.1.0. iOS declares ExpoModulesCore. Actual Gradle resolution in a standalone build has not been inspected; npm audit cannot establish this graph. Apache lists Commons IO2.x before2.14 as affected by CVE-2024-47554 in XmlStreamReader (https://commons.apache.org/proper/commons-io/security.html); the reviewed picker uses FilenameUtils.getExtension, not that XML reader, but this is not a blanket absence-of-reachability proof. Resolve and audit the complete native dependency graph before private/production use; no clean-native-dependency claim. Expo Go has its own prebuilt graph, distinct from the unchanged standalone APK.

## ADR025 — bounded counterevidence with durable publication obligations

W05 / V6-F01/F03/F15: use `counterevidence.v1` behind default-off `STRUCTURED_CHALLENGE_ENABLED`. Capture up to six initially supported claim targets for one consequential question, including their original revisions, scopes and evidence digest, before one admitted public challenge search, durable reads and scoped support reassessment. Original targets survive fresh extractor omission. Typed outcomes distinguish found counterevidence, none found in inspected evidence, blocked, unresolved at limit and unknown; no counterexample found is never universal proof. This selects a bounded target set, not every report claim.

Impact checklist: the additive, idempotent migration030 owns `counterevidence_checks` and `runs.counterevidence_required_revision`; contracts/core own the strict action/search schemas and deterministic selection/outcomes, backend modules own restoration and worker orchestration. No new dependency, service, model route, prompt, connector or monetary allowance. The transformed search accepts only an original-question provenance span plus the closed suffix `contradictions limitations exceptions`; validate the actual executable query before admission. Private/source/target text cannot expand public-query authority. Existing consent, mixed-attachment restriction, three-search ceiling, account/project/key reservations and receipt rules remain binding. Search body/digest logic is shared through the existing search port; adapter composition stays in backend runtime.

Target capture atomically sets the required revision. Publication independently restores original extraction/support and current search/read/support proof; missing required records, stale or tampered evidence and optimistic model judgments cannot erase the obligation. Contradicted, qualified and unresolved targets produce localized canonical limitations even when fresh extraction omits them. Restart excludes challenge searches from ordinary discovery and reuses finished reads without network. Unknown provider outcomes retain holds and cannot be blindly resent. Account deletion purges challenge content before its raw/derived provenance.

Regression linkage: core counterevidence controls and `model-gateway.integration.test.ts` cover contradictory/qualified/supportive evidence, omitted original targets, event-only/missing proof, forged revisions/results, private-query rejection, restart, concurrent issuance, shared ceilings, unknown search/model receipts, stale cancellation and deletion. Session-transition evidence records the initial type/test/restart failures and final 305/305 PostgreSQL plus 148/99/61/6 verify passes. These use synthetic evidence and fabricated provider transports; they establish local boundaries, not live semantics or independent quality. The separate extraction suite and actual native research journey remain distinct evidence classes. Report-challenge POST remains feedback storage; it does not schedule requested verification.

Rollback sets `STRUCTURED_CHALLENGE_ENABLED=false` to stop new scheduling (or disables structured processing more broadly). Retain required-proof markers/readers, publication vetoes, deletion, receipt settlement and unknown holds; do not drop migration030 or erase admitted obligations. Engineering lead owns the flag; review at the W08 matched-evaluation checkpoint before activation or removal. Persisted typed outcomes provide audit telemetry; no private target text enters ordinary logs. No paid activation is authorized by this decision.

ADR025 review amendment: limited outcomes must pass independent challenge restoration and carry every target warning; lost required records veto every outcome because missing targets cannot establish safe qualifications. Request-digest recovery closes the search-receipt/challenge-pointer crash gap without another query. Markdown sharing retains canonical limitations. Reproduced negative controls and passing final focused results are preserved in verification/v6/checkpoint-review/.

## ADR028 — recover provider costs without resending work

Historical repair only reclassifies demonstrably unbacked estimate-equals-confirmed rows in terminal live runs with known settlement amounts. Test-only legacy key contamination is isolated after assertions; production guard remains strict.
