## What is not defensible
No evidence here establishes that our unbuilt system beats any of these products. No official feature page proves user satisfaction. No unavailable public documentation proves a feature is absent. No forum post about ordinary chat/Claude Code establishes a failure of the vendor’s dedicated Research mode.

The strongest near-term differentiation is an implementation quality claim on a narrow workflow: decisive evidence under hard constraints, safe low-repair corrections, and inspectable mobile results. Competitors may already perform these behaviors well; the proposed experiment must test that possibility.

## Currentness discipline
Capability and complaint dates are kept separately. Perplexity’s September 15 documentation materially changes how old complaints should be interpreted. A claimed early-2026 regression cannot be presumed current. Grok’s July 2025 control-location complaint has a reported workaround; it is historical design evidence, not current competitive weakness.

For each future competitor run record plan, locale, platform/build, mode, source permissions, question/files, time/date and outcome. Never compare a dedicated research workflow to ordinary chat because it is easier to win. Exact subscription prices are deliberately omitted: they are unnecessary to this capability judgment and were not measured as comparable purchase offers.
