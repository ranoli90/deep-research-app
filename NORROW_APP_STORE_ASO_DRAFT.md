## Description

Make your next decision with evidence, not another wall of text.

Norrow helps you research a question, compare your options, and explore the sources behind an answer. Start with what you are trying to figure out. Add your requirements or a supported document, then follow the evidence that matters to you.

RESEARCH YOUR ACTUAL QUESTION
Explain your goal in everyday language. Add a budget, preference, location, or other requirement so the investigation stays focused on your task.

COMPARE WHAT MATTERS
Look beyond a generic list. Explore the differences, trade-offs, and unresolved questions that can affect your choice.

OPEN THE EVIDENCE
Tap citations to inspect the supporting source passages. See where information comes from and where the available evidence leaves uncertainty.

BRING YOUR DOCUMENTS
Ask questions about supported digital PDFs and text documents. Document-reading limits are shown when the available text is incomplete.

KEEP THE CONVERSATION GOING
Ask for an explanation, change a requirement, or investigate a point more deeply. Return to saved research instead of starting from a blank page every time.

Built for reading and checking research on your phone, with the answer first and the detail there when you need it.

AI-generated answers can be incomplete or wrong. Check important sources and use appropriate professional advice for high-stakes decisions. Research capabilities and usage depend on the available service and your account allowance.
