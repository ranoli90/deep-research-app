# Product specification — revision 2
Owner role: product lead. Status: proposed product decisions, not validated demand. Reviewed: 2026-09-16.

## Promise
Research that helps the user complete a decision with less checking, correcting and repeating work. Long reports remain available when requested; concision is not the only objective. The interface stays calm and familiar while the system preserves constraints and traceable evidence.

## Initial focus
**JOB-1: Compare technical options under hard constraints.** Examples are selecting software, equipment or a technical service when version compatibility, geography, portability, deployment mode, real total cost or an exclusion can eliminate an otherwise attractive choice. The output is an eligibility-aware comparison with the decisive evidence and unresolved blockers, not a generic category guide.

**JOB-2: Reconcile supplied documents with public evidence.** A user brings manuals, proposals, claims or specifications and needs to know which statements apply, disagree or remain unsupported. The output is a concise answer with exact supporting sections, scoped contradictions and reproducible calculations. Uploaded source quality is visible; scanned tables are not silently treated as fully read.

**RETURN JOURNEY: Revisit the same decision.** The user changes a constraint, adds a document, disputes a claim or asks what changed. Reuse valid work, but reopen candidate discovery when the decision space changes. Do not mistake a cheap update that preserves a now-wrong candidate set for success.

This focus is a testable beachhead, not proven willingness to pay. Public complaints justify the problem class but do not establish a commercially sufficient audience. Recruit actual users with recent examples before fixing retail pricing or acquisition claims.

## Journey contracts
| Journey | Trigger and inputs | Useful result and evidence standard | Failure consequence | Waiting/return hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| JOB-1 | A pending choice; constraints; candidates optional | Feasible/infeasible/unknown statuses, source-backed distinguishing facts and an explicitly conditional conclusion | Wrong purchase, rework or missed compatible option | Test tolerance at 2-, 5- and 10-minute scenarios; these are research-study conditions, not delivery promises. Return when constraints/options change. |
| JOB-2 | Conflicting documents/claims plus question and date | Scoped claim comparison; page/section evidence; calculations with units; missing-access disclosure | Misreading a proposal/manual; wasted checking | Test small-document completion separately from web-heavy tasks. Return with a new version or challenged passage. |
| RETURN | “Actually…” correction, new source or refresh request | Changed findings, unchanged evidence retained, affected work reopened, clear version lineage | Stale recommendation presented as current | The update must be materially less repair work than a full restart without correctness loss. |

## Before, during and after
Before research, infer only reversible noncritical assumptions, display consequential assumptions in one compact brief, and ask at most the necessary questions. Never force a plan-confirmation screen for a clear task. Show the expected allowance/cost policy before spending; no model/provider controls by default.

During research, show factual stages and a short statement of the central unresolved question. Corrections use the ordinary composer. A changed brief marks affected output as being checked. Avoid popping six technical controls onto the screen. Adding a private source preserves explicit processing scope and query privacy.

When blocked, say what is missing, what was actually attempted and how much of the answer remains useful. Distinguish “cannot access the required evidence” from “no option exists.” Offer one relevant next action, not an endless request for clarification.

At completion, put the answer first, then why it meets the task, disqualifying constraints, unresolved matters, and expandable evidence. For exploratory work, an informative synthesis is valid; do not force every question into a buying decision. Uncertainty is local to affected claims.

On return, restore stable reading position, source context and latest report status. Let the user inspect an older version while clearly identifying stale conclusions. Privacy deletion may remove affected content from older versions; version history is not an exemption from deletion.

## Launch scope
Native iPhone and Android, English interface, Research as the primary surface with Library reachable from Menu/Profile (not a required bottom tab), profile Settings, sign-in/consent, URLs and supported text/PDF attachments, durable runs, saved reports, citation sheet, cancellation, one-claim challenge, corrected-task rerun, native sharing of Markdown/text, redacted diagnostics and deletion.

Start with technically supported digital PDFs/text/Markdown. Additional formats and reliable table/visual extraction are gated by extraction tests. Do not advertise full-document understanding when only text or an abstract was accessed. PDF export is optional until native/export evidence is tested; no dead export button.

General research outside the two launch jobs may remain available with honest access and evidence limits. No launch claim of professional systematic-review completeness, clinical diagnosis, legal determinations, investment suitability or guaranteed exhaustive discovery. No outbound actions, purchases, social network, permanent inferred personal profile, enterprise connectors or unrestricted browser agent in the first release.

## Innovation hypotheses
| ID and priority | Behavior the customer notices | Classification | Mechanism | Hardest objection and decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 — first | Finds the fact that rules an option in/out rather than repeating summaries | Differentiated implementation hypothesis, not a claimed invention | Decision-sensitive gap registry, scoped feasibility checks and source-type escalation | The gap planner can miss unknown unknowns. Keep a bounded counterexample/candidate-discovery pass and test against independently identified decisive evidence. Reject complexity if gold-evidence tests show synthesis, not retrieval, is the bottleneck. |
| I2 — second | A correction yields a trustworthy update without repeating all work | Differentiated implementation hypothesis | Typed dependencies, selection-space invalidation, revision fences and a semantic change report | Dependency maintenance may cost more than rereading small tasks. Use full bounded recomputation for small/ambiguous changes and retain incremental mode only if measured cost/repair benefit survives correctness checks. |
| I3 — alongside baseline | Sees the useful answer and checks its decisive passage quickly on a phone | Useful product refinement | Canonical report blocks, local uncertainty, stable source/scroll anchors | A summary can hide nuance. Derive every summary assertion from the same claim set, preserve detailed view and measure understanding rather than liking alone. |

Existing alternatives: ChatGPT and Perplexity document steering/report controls; Claude documents iterative research; NotebookLM offers persistent source-grounded research; Elicit offers specialized evidence workflows. These observations rule out claiming that the generic features are novel. See S01–S09 and `research/COMPETITOR_REVIEW.md`.

## Deferred and rejected
Defer scheduled monitoring until a return-use signal and bounded refresh policy exist; the review creates no automation. Defer cross-project personalization and broad connectors until explicit consent/revocation/deletion are tested. Defer browser-heavy retrieval until text tools demonstrably miss valuable accessible sources.

Reject mandatory swarms, model-majority “verification,” confidence percentages without calibration, minimum source/word quotas, automatically longer answers, decorative progress and claims that opaque provider research was inspected internally. These are not substitutes for user outcomes.
